After #63 (walnut navigation rail, compact rail below 1180 px, Organs as start page, EN/DE and scheme toggles, shared page head). Orientation (H1), consistency (H7) and character (H9) each gain one point: every page now has the same rail, a serif page title and the primary action at the top right, and the walnut rail with paper-coloured active item reads as an instrument rather than an admin template. The Organ editor (#64), Tape view (#65), lists (#66) and Tape import (#69) are not restyled yet, so density, data entry, feedback and the Tape as hero are unchanged.

## Findings
1. **[sev 3] H6 · 08-tape-view, 11-tape-view-dark** — The Tape is still small (3 drawn Columns, about 240 px wide) and sits under a text block and a bullet list. It has no millimetre scale, no Column numbers and no paper-tape look, and the page head has no Organ name, back link or export action. The dark scheme inverts it well (light Holes on near-black, legible), but the picture is the same. Fix: make the Tape the visual centre, scaled to true mm with a ruler, move "What changed on import" into a side panel, and put the Organ and the actions in the page head. Tag: #65.
2. **[sev 3] H3 · 04-organ-editor-filled, 10-organ-editor-german** — 31 Pipe rows plus six stacked Tape Bed fields still make a page about 2640 px tall. Each Tape Bed field is 800 px wide for 2-3 digit values, and the rail leaves 1000+ px of the window unused on the right. Fix: Tape Bed and Pipes side by side, narrow numeric fields, compact rows, two or three Pipes per row, a sticky Save bar. Tag: #64.
3. **[sev 3] H5 · 03-organ-editor-invalid** — Save is disabled with no explanation. Empty fields show only their label and no inline error, and Delete Organ sits next to Save on an unsaved Organ. The page head, which now holds the primary action on the lists, holds nothing here. Fix: inline "Please enter a value" errors after touch, hide Delete for a New Organ, move Save into the page head or a sticky bar and separate the destructive action. Tag: #64.
4. **[sev 2] H4/H7 · 03, 07, 10** — Stacked outlined fields touch each other with no gap; the floating-label cut-out of each field overlaps the border of the field above (Tape Bed, the 31 MIDI-note fields, the import fields), most visibly in German (10: "Rand (mm)", "Lochdurchmesser (mm)"). Empty fields in 03 look like placeholders rather than missing values. Fix: add a vertical gap or a two-column grid with `subscriptSizing: dynamic`. Tag: #64 (editor) and #69 (import).
5. **[sev 2] H2 · 02-organ-list-empty, 06-tape-list-empty** — Organs is now the start page, but its empty state is only the line "No Organs yet." in the top left, with the rest of the page blank. The primary button is in the head, but nothing explains the first step or that a Tape needs an Organ. Fix: a guided empty state (1. Describe an Organ, 2. Bring a Song onto a Tape) with the button in the middle, and on Tapes without Organs a link to New Organ. Tag: #66.
6. **[sev 2] H7 · 05-organ-list, 09-tape-list** — Lists are plain text rows. Organ rows show name and "31 Pipes" with a bare trash icon; Tape rows show bold name, Organ, "24 Columns" and "60 mm" with no labels, and Open is a bare text link. Deleting has no visible confirmation, and the rows do not look like the cards the rail and head promise. Fix: cards with labelled facts, Open and Delete everywhere, a confirm step for Delete. Tag: #66.
7. **[sev 2] H8 · 12-organ-list-compact** — **new.** The compact rail (below 1180 px) shows icons only: the two nav items have no visible label, and the language and scheme toggles stack as three and two bare segments (A/sun/moon icons) with no text. Fix: add tooltips and aria-labels to the nav items and toggles (check in the capture's DOM), keep the active pill, and lift "Saved on this computer" (about 13 px, grey on walnut, low contrast, hidden when compact) to at least 4.5:1. Tag: new.
8. **[sev 2] H4 · 10-organ-editor-german** — The Minimum Hole Length shows "2.5" with a dot while its neighbours show "2,5" (Mindestlochlänge). Fix: format every number with the locale. Tag: #64.
9. **[sev 2] H2 · 07-tape-import** — The chosen file is not shown as such: the word "Lead" next to the button is the Part name, not a file chip. "Use proposal" is unexplained, the fit result is a plain bullet list with no warning state for Unplayable Notes > 0, and the Parts fieldset touches the next field. Fix: show the file name as a chip, put the fit in a result card, explain the proposal and warn when Unplayable Notes is above 0. Tag: #69.
10. **[sev 2] H8 · 04-organ-editor-filled, 08-tape-view** — The up, down and delete icon buttons have no visible label (check tooltips and aria-labels), and the disabled arrow and the disabled Save are low contrast. Hint text and the note labels (F3) are about 12 px. Fix: tooltips and aria-labels, at least 12 px for all helper text, check 4.5:1 for disabled-but-informative text. Tag: #64 (editor) and #65 (Tape).

Also seen, sev 1: the leave-unsaved dialog (03b) is clear (Stay is the filled primary, Discard changes outlined, each problem listed) but words the Pipe error differently ("Enter the MIDI note of this Pipe.") from the others ("Please enter a value."). Tag: #64.

## Strengths
- The walnut rail with a paper-coloured active item and a serif Crancula wordmark gives the app a craft character; the active page is unmistakable on every screen (H1, H9).
- One layout everywhere: rail, serif page title, primary action at the top right (New Organ, Bring a song onto a tape). The left edge of the title and the content now align (H7).
- The rail holds the always-visible settings: EN/DE and the scheme toggle (auto, light, dark), plus a "Saved on this computer" note that tells the owner nothing is sent anywhere. German nav labels (Drehorgeln, Lochbänder) and the footer note fit without breaking.
- The dark scheme (11) is consistent: ink-black page, light text, light Holes on dark with visible Column lines, and the rail stays walnut.
- The compact rail (12) keeps the page head and primary action intact at 1024 px and gives the content most of the width.
- The core flow is still short: New Organ, Bring a Song onto a Tape, Create Tape, Open. The Tapes empty state has a clear primary button.
- Plain Material with tokens and shipped fonts: the remaining restyle is fully feasible with minimal SCSS (H10).
